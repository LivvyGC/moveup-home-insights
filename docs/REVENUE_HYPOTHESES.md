# Revenue hypotheses — untested

1. **Free analysis.** Interview and observe 20 renters with a near-term renewal. Proposed success threshold: 12 complete a scenario without assistance and 8 identify a concrete next action.
2. **One-time paid summary.** Test stated interest at **$9, $19, $29** without taking payments. Proposed success threshold: 25% click the feedback link and at least 5 interviewees explain why they would pay. These are hypothetical metrics, not conversion claims.
3. **Organization pilot.** Interview five campus housing offices, tenant organizations or credit unions about a **$50–$100/month** pilot. Proposed threshold: one dated written pilot commitment. Pricing and demand are unvalidated.
