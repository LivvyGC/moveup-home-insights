# Renewal Check — decision flow and unresolved limits

Reviewed 2026-11-14. This branch changes no user accounts or backend storage.

## New flow

1. Four-field renewal intake and explicit submit.
2. Actual increase/decrease in monthly and selected-horizon dollars.
3. Conditional action based on *potential* rent-law concerns, not above-market pricing.
4. State and local rent standards shown independently; no certified total legal ceiling.
5. New-lease rent benchmarks, no averages; Apartment List 1BR/2BR values extracted from founder-uploaded Oct. 2026 CSV.
6. Stay and optional move/buy scenarios; incomplete assumptions explicitly marked.
7. One-page print-friendly report with original government/research links.

## Material limits

- This app does not determine statutory eligibility or establish that a rent increase is illegal. A user's self-reported coverage is not a legal verification.
- City names alone do not prove that a property lies within municipal jurisdiction. Verify the actual unit address and authority before filing.
- California statewide rent caps, local rent stabilization, prior increases, banked adjustments, exemptions, and authorization rules can overlap. The app displays **separate published standards** instead of choosing an unjustified lowest cap.
- For Seattle, date a written notice was received may identify a possible 180-day issue; this also depends on the kind of increase and exceptions.
- Rent market values reflect asking/new-lease trends; Zillow's overall index is not bedroom-matched. Apartment List 1BR/2BR data are provided where available.
- Optional buy comparisons exclude important expense categories until supplied, and lack resale or appreciation assumptions. Optional move comparison excludes moving costs until entered. Avoid interpreting partial totals as complete.
- Existing raw rent datasets are founder-supplied and the demo-use permission for Apartment List is founder-confirmed; commercial redistribution is not independently verified.
- Data are from Sep/Oct 2026; the app shows an over-45-day warning per research source.
- No form URL has been supplied, so no paid-conversion capture is linked.
- Browser screenshot and printed-paper QA remain to be completed before public release.
