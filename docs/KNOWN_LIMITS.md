# Known limitations and skeptical self-review

- This is a **demo**, not a legal determination or a broker/lender quote.
- Rental statistics measure new leases or asking-rent indexes, not the price a particular landlord will offer. Zillow and Apartment List have different methodologies and dates.
- User confirmed demo authorization for Apartment List; commercial redistribution and Zillow rights are not independently verified.
- Local rent law coverage depends on unit age, property type, location, written notices, tenancy history and potentially authorized exceptions/banked increases. The app uses conditional **standard** adjustments, not an authoritative legal ceiling for a specific tenant.
- Seattle 2027 cap is kept as a separate dated record. Earlier/later dates may be unavailable.
- The optional home purchase view does not include property tax, insurance, HOA, maintenance or closing costs unless manually entered. It is a cash-outflow comparison; ownership also changes assets and liabilities.
- The mortgage rate defaults to the national Freddie Mac PMMS value dated 2026-11-12; not a personalized or guaranteed rate.
- No resale proceeds, appreciation, rental inflation forecasts, capital gains or tax treatment are calculated.
- Source snapshot updates require new dated input files. No auto-refresh and no automatic city-law validation.
- The feedback form is intentionally not linked until the founder gives a real URL.
- Data staleness is computed from observation dates, not the founder's upload timestamp.
- Building-year input is advisory; it does not automatically certify coverage.
- Source-tag tests inspect known UI render patterns, not a full browser accessibility audit.
