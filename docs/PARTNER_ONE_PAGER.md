# MoveUp Renewal Check

**For a campus housing office, tenant support organization or credit union**

Renters facing a renewal must compare a time-sensitive rent offer, market alternatives and possible legal protections. MoveUp provides one browser-based comparison with clearly attributed rental benchmarks from Zillow ZORI and Apartment List; links to official rental-policy guidance; and matched-horizon stay, move and buy expense scenarios.

**The demo experience:** renter enters their own offer, checks whether reported rules might apply, adjusts costs, and prints a one-page summary. No login or backend storage of personal details.

**Boundaries:** market asking-rent indexes are not forecasts of an individual renewal. Legal coverage is not certified. Buying comparisons may omit taxes, insurance and resale considerations unless entered.

**Pilot request:** Try it with five real renewal cases and report where the data, calculations or eligibility flow mislead or save time.

**Business hypothesis:** test a $50–$100 monthly organization pilot. No price or buyer willingness has been validated.
