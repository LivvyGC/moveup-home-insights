# Interviews

## Renters
1. When does your lease end?
2. What increase have you been offered?
3. How much time do you have to respond?
4. What would you do without this product?
5. How did you compare other rentals?
6. Have you checked rent-protection eligibility?
7. What information was difficult to find?
8. Which inputs here were difficult?
9. What concrete decision would this report change?
10. Would you pay $9, $19 or $29 for the printable report, and why?

## Partners
1. How often do people ask for lease-renewal guidance?
2. What workflow and tools handle that now?
3. What accuracy/privacy requirements would block a pilot?
4. What do you pay for current tools, and would a $50–$100 monthly test be worth considering?
